# Hotel Booking Analysis Insights

## 1. Preferred Hotel Type
- **City Hotels** are preferred by **62.33%** of customers, while **Resort Hotels** account for **37.67%**.
- **Insight:** Higher demand for urban hotels, possibly due to business travel or city tourism.

## 2. Monthly Booking Lead Time Trends
- **Resort Hotels** have higher lead times, peaking around **August (128 days)**.
- **City Hotels** have a lower lead time, peaking around **June (114 days)**.
- **Insight:** Guests book resort hotels much earlier, likely due to vacation planning.

## 3. Meal Preferences
- **Bed & Breakfast (BB)** is the most popular meal choice.
- **Resort Hotels** see more guests choosing **Half Board (HB) or Full Board (FB)**, indicating a preference for all-inclusive options.

## 4. Top 7 Countries by Number of Guests
- **Portugal (PRT)** contributes the highest number of guests (**48,656**), followed by:
  - **GBR (20,488)**
  - **FRA (17,772)**
- **Insight:** Significant international customer base, with Portugal leading guest arrivals.

## 5. Peak Booking Year
- **2016** had the highest number of bookings (**26,379**), particularly in **City Hotels**.
- **Insight:** Suggests 2016 was a high-demand year, possibly due to events or seasonal trends.

## 6. Average ADR (Average Daily Rate) by Month
- **ADR peaks in July & August**, with **City Hotels** having higher rates overall.
- **Resort Hotels** maintain slightly lower but consistent ADR during summer months.

## 7. Cancellation Trends
- Most cancellations occur through **TA/TO (Travel Agencies / Tour Operators): 66,416**.
- **Corporate & Direct** channels see fewer cancellations, indicating more committed bookings.
- **GDS (Global Distribution System)** and **Undefined** channels have minimal cancellations.

## 8. Repeated Guests Analysis
- **City Hotels** have the highest number of repeated guests, especially through the **Corporate** channel.
- **Resort Hotels** have fewer repeated guests but maintain consistency across **Direct & TA/TO** channels.
- **Insight:** Business travelers are more loyal to City Hotels, while Resort Hotels attract one-time vacationers.
